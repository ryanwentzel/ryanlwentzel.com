We're living in days of upheaval and uncertainty...

## What is your only comfort in life and in death?

Times of trouble present us with the opportunity to remember what our ultimate confidence is as Christians. "Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God" (Ps 20:7).  

The Heidelberg Catechism beautifully expresses the basis of our confidence.

> **What is your only comfort in life and in death?**  
> That I am not my own,  
> but belong --- body and soul, in life and in death ---  
> to my faithful Savior, Jesus Christ.  
> He has fully paid the price for all my sins with his precious blood,  
> and has delivered me from the tyranny of the devil.  
> He also watches over me in such a way  
> that not a hair can fall from my head  
> without the will of my Father in heaven;  
> in fact, all things must work together for my salvation.  
>  
> Because I belong to him,  
> Christ, by his Holy Spirit,  
> also assures me of eternal life  
> and makes me wholeheartedly willing and ready  
> from now on to live for him.  

Andy Crouch [recently wrote](https://journal.praxislabs.org/love-in-the-time-of-coronavirus-26aaeb0396e3) that, "One of the great opportunities of this crisis is the chance to relearn these words, teach them to our children and to new Christians for the first time, and live them out together."

This is a time to ensure we've built our lives on the firm foundation of Jesus Christ --- his blood and his righteousness.
