I am a husband, father of five, and a pastor at Grace Bible Church (GBC) in Escondido, CA.


I have been a pastor at GBC since 2009. Before coming on staff full-time in 2016, I worked as a software engineer at a large biotech company in San Diego. My primary roles at GBC include pastoral care, preaching, and teaching.
