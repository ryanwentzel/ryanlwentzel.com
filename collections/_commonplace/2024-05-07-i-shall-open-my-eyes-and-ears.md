Clyde Kilby made a list of resolutions designed to promote his mental health. Here's number six:

> I shall open my eyes and ears. Once every day I shall simply stare at a tree, a flower, a cloud, or a person. I shall not then be concerned at all to ask what they are, but simply be glad that they are. I shall joyfully allow them the mystery of what Lewis calls their "divine, magical, terrifying, and ecstatic" existence.

— Clyde Kilby in ["10 Resolutions for Mental Health"](https://www.desiringgod.org/articles/10-resolutions-for-mental-health) by John Piper
