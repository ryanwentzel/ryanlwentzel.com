One of the beautiful things about a church is its diversity. It's full of people with different backgrounds, life experiences, and perspectives. This diverse community comes together as one family in Jesus Christ, worshiping the same God and following the same Savior.

One of the challenging things about a church is its diversity. Our differences have the potential to cause conflict. The gospel of Jesus Christ binds us together, but we work out the implications of our faith in different ways. We don't see eye-to-eye on a range of secondary issues.

How do we get along with fellow Christians despite our differences? Ryan seeks to answer that question in this sermon on Romans 14:1–12.

*This sermon was delivered on January 28, 2024, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California.*
