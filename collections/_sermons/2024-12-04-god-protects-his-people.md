The gospel doesn't offer us an escape from pain — that's not what God promises us. What the gospel does provide is help and hope in the midst of trials.

Listen as Ryan walks through 1 Samuel 19, showing how it ultimately points to the hope and help the gospel of Jesus Christ provides.

*This sermon was preached on October 6, 2024, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California.*
