1 Samuel 3:1–4:1a is a charming story about the LORD calling the boy Samuel to be his prophet. But that's not all. 

In this sermon, I explore how the story is ultimately about the God who speaks through his prophets, his written Word, and supremely in his Son, Jesus Christ.

*This sermon was preached on May 26, 2024, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California.*
