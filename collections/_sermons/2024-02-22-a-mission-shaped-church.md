In this sermon on Romans 15:14–33, Ryan explores three characteristics of a mission-shaped church. A mission-shaped church:

1. Is grounded in the gospel
2. Reaches the unreached
3. Builds gospel partnerships

*This sermon was preached on February 18, 2024, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California.*
