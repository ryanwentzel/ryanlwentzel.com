How do you deal with your anger, outrage, or hatred? Vent it? Suppress it? Ignore it? Most of us struggle with knowing how to handle these emotions. 

However, the Psalms teach us a way to engage our anger faithfully. They invite us to pray our hate. 

In this talk, Ryan explains why and how to do it.

*This talk was given on July 2, 2023, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California*.
