## A Pilgrim Prayer

<figure>
	<img src="https://cdn.ryanlwentzel.com/assets/img/content/lebasque-le-village-md.webp" alt="Henri Lebasque">
	<figcaption>
		Henri Lebasque, <em>Le Village</em>
	</figcaption>
</figure>

*The following prayer comes from the Sarum Breviary, an English book of worship developed by Osmund, Bishop of Salisbury, in the late 11th century. Thomas Cranmer drew heavily from this medieval liturgical work when he compiled the first* Book of Common Prayer *in 1549.*

<div class="dinkus">* * *</div>

> Almighty God, we call on you, the fountain of everlasting light, and ask you to send out your truth into our hearts and pour out on us the glory of your brightness; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

**Web**

- **[Something Holy Shines](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2025/01/poetry-christianity-imagination-shakespeare-coleridge-malcolm-guite/) by Malcolm Guite**. How the poetic imagination "lifts the veil and restores our sense of wonder."

- **[Am I Too Soft for This Angry World?](https://www.sagechristianity.com/articles-essays/i-am-resolving-to-stay-soft) by Jessica Fadel** at [Sage Christianity](https://www.sagechristianity.com/). Why "softness" might help you better follow Jesus.

- **[On Art & Suffering: How artists help us to name the pain in our lives](https://wdavidotaylor.substack.com/p/on-art-and-suffering) by W. David O. Taylor**. *"Artists...are especially equipped to prepare us to face our own struggles with humility and to feel our own sorrows deeply without being undone by them, and to feel the possibility of hope, not just on the other side of our sorrows but also in the very middle of them."*

**Books**

- ***[This is Happiness](https://amzn.to/3CSsiZJ)* by Niall Williams.** I don't remember where I saw this novel recommended, but it’s delightful!

- ***[Zero at the Bone: Fifty Entries Against Despair](https://amzn.to/3X5RuD8)* by Christian Wiman**. Wiman is a poet and author living with incurable cancer. His reflections on life and faith are never simplistic.

- ***[Another Day: Sabbath Poems 2013—2023](https://amzn.to/4hCzKaJ)* by Wendell Berry**. Mr. Berry is now 90 years old, and we're not likely to see another volume of poems from his pen. I'm savoring this one.

## A Poem

<figure>
	<img src="https://cdn.ryanlwentzel.com/assets/img/content/barney-the-field-md.webp" alt="Painting by Alice Pike Barney titled 'The Field'">
	<figcaption>Alice Pike Barney, <cite>The Field</cite></figcaption>
</figure>

**The Bright Field**\
By R.S. Thomas

I have seen the sun break through\
to illuminate a small field \
for a while, and gone my way \
and forgotten it. But that was the \
pearl of great price, the one field that had \
treasure in it. I realise now \
that I must give all that I have \
to possess it. Life is not hurrying

on to a receding future, nor hankering after \
an imagined past. It is the turning \
aside like Moses to the miracle \
of the lit bush, to a brightness \
that seemed as transitory as your youth \
once, but is the eternity that awaits you.
