Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*, which I explain in the [first issue](https://buttondown.email/ryanwentzel/archive/who-is-tom-bombadil-and-why-are-we-in-his-house/).

I write about things I'm reading or thinking about. You can check out the [archives](https://buttondown.email/ryanwentzel/archive/) to get a feel for the content.
