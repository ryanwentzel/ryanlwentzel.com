I've been a pastor at [Grace Bible Church](https://grcbible.org/) in Escondido, California since 2009. Before joining the GBC staff full-time in 2016, I worked as a software engineer at a large biotech company in San Diego. 

My primary roles at GBC include preaching, teaching, and pastoral care.
