After a few wrong turns (I tried navigating without Google Maps' turn-by-turn navigation ☺️), I found the parking area at the San Elijo Lagoon Ecological Reserve's La Orilla Trailhead. Stepping out of my truck, I was met with more humidity than anticipated. The sky above was a dull gray, typical for a late-August morning in coastal North County San Diego. The temperature was in the mid-sixties.

!["Route map"](https://d1yo597syf8yny.cloudfront.net/images/content/birding-route-20210830.jpg "Route map")

My watch showed 7:23 A.M. when I set out on the La Orilla Trail. Immediately, mosquitos began to eat me alive (they continued to pester me until I reached the [Santa Helena Trail](https://thenaturecollective.org/location/santa-helena-trail/")). Despite the swarms of insects, I stopped to snap photos of thickets of Desert Grape lining the trail.
