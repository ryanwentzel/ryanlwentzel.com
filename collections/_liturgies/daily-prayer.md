## Opening Sentence

And the glory of the LORD shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see it together, for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.  
**— Isaiah 40:5**

## Adoration

Praise God from whom all blessings flow;  
Praise him all creatures here below;  
Praise him above you heavenly host;  
Praise Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. **Amen**.  
**— Doxology**

## Confession of Sin

Almighty God,  
&emsp;unto whom all hearts are open,  
&emsp;all desires known,  
&emsp;and from whom no secrets are hidden—  
cleanse the thoughts of our hearts  
&emsp;by the inspiration of your Holy Spirit,  
&emsp;so that we may perfectly love you,  
&emsp;and worthily magnify your holy name;  
&emsp;through Christ our Lord. Amen.  
**— The Book of Common Prayer**

## Assurance of Pardon

Come now, let us reason together, says the LORD: though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they are red like crimson, they shall become like wool.  
**— Isaiah 1:18**

## Praise

Glory to the Father,  
&emsp;and to the Son,  
&emsp;and to the Holy Spirit:    
As it was in the beginning,  
&emsp;is now, and ever shall be,  
&emsp;world without end. Amen.  
**— Gloria Patri**

## The Lord's Prayer

Our Father in heaven,  
hallowed be your name;  
your kingdom come;  
your will be done,  
&emsp;on earth as it is in heaven.  
Give us this day our daily bread.  
And forgive us our debts,  
&emsp;as we forgive our debtors.  
And lead us not into temptation  
&emsp;but deliver us from evil.  
For yours is the kingdom, and the power,  
and the glory, forever. Amen.

## Personal Prayer

Your glorious Majesty surround me,  
&emsp;the blessed Trinity protect me,  
&emsp;and the eternal Godhead preserve me.  
Your unlimited mercy support me;  
&emsp;your loving kindness encompass me;  
&emsp;your favor make me to rejoice.  
The eternal truth of God be my delight,  
&emsp;the saving knowledge of Christ    
&emsp;&emsp;strengthen me,  
&emsp;and the all-prevailing grace of God  
&emsp;&emsp;be sufficient for me.  
May the grace of God the Father lead me,  
&emsp;the wisdom of God the Son  
&emsp;&emsp;be my consolation,  
&emsp;and the power of the Holy Spirit  
&emsp;&emsp;enlighten me.  
Lord my Creator, stand by me;  
&emsp;my Redeemer, save me;  
&emsp;and my Comforter, dwell with me.  
Amen.  
**— Johann Habermann**

## Grace

May the LORD  
&emsp;bless me and keep me.  
May the LORD  
&emsp;make his face to shine upon me  
&emsp;and be gracious to me.  
May the LORD  
&emsp;lift up his countenance upon me  
&emsp;and give me peace.  
**— Based on Numbers 6:24–26**
