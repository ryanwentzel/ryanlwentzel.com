## Opening Sentence

Hear my cry, O God,  
&emsp;listen to my prayer;   
from the end of the earth I call to you   
&emsp;when my heart is faint.   
Lead me to the rock   
&emsp;that is higher than I,   
for you have been my refuge,   
&emsp;a strong tower against the enemy.  
**— Psalm 61:1–3**

## Adoration

Praise God from whom all blessings flow;  
Praise him all creatures here below;  
Praise him above you heavenly host;  
Praise Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. Amen.  
**— Doxology**


## Psalm

*Read the psalm for the day.*

## Praise

Glory to the Father,  
&emsp;and to the Son,  
&emsp;and to the Holy Spirit:    
As it was in the beginning,  
&emsp;is now, and ever shall be,  
&emsp;world without end. Amen.  
**— Gloria Patri**

## The Lord's Prayer

Our Father in heaven,  
hallowed be your name;  
your kingdom come;  
your will be done,  
&emsp;on earth as it is in heaven.  
Give us this day our daily bread.  
And forgive us our debts,  
&emsp;as we forgive our debtors.  
And lead us not into temptation  
&emsp;but deliver us from evil.  
For yours is the kingdom, and the power,  
and the glory, forever. Amen.

## Personal Prayer

*Pray for your needs, as well as those of your family, friends, church, and neighborhood.*

## Grace

May the LORD  
&emsp;bless us and keep us.  
May the LORD  
&emsp;make his face to shine upon us  
&emsp;and be gracious to us.  
May the LORD  
&emsp;lift up his countenance upon us  
&emsp;and give us peace.  
**— Based on Numbers 6:24–26**
