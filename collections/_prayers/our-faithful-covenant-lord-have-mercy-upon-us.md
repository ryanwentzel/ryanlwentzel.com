Our faithful covenant Lord, you who showed mercy to your wayward people Israel time and again, have mercy upon us. Remember your covenant with us in Jesus Christ. Forgive us our sins, that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise, through Christ our Savior. Amen.
