My gracious God and Father, you who uphold my life, by your almighty power, keep me through all that I experience today — sorrows and joys, challenges and opportunities, disappointments and blessings — so that I might give thanks to you in every circumstance. I pray this in the strong name of my Savior, Jesus Christ. Amen.
