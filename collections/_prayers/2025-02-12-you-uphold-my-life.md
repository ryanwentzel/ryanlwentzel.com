My gracious God and Father, you who uphold my life by your almighty power, keep me through all that this day brings — sorrows and joys, challenges and opportunities, disappointments and blessings — that in every circumstance, I may rest in your unfailing love. I pray this in the strong name of my Savior, Jesus Christ. Amen.
