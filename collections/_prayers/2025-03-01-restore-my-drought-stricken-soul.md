*And now, God, do it again—*\
*&emsp;bring rains to our drought-stricken lives*\
*So those who planted their crops in despair*\
*&emsp; will shout “Yes!” at the harvest,*\
*So those who went off with heavy hearts*\
*&emsp; will come home laughing, with armloads of blessing.*\
— Psalm 126:4–6 (*The Message*)

O Lord, you who water the desert and robe it in a riot of color, let your life-giving rains restore my drought-stricken soul, that I may laugh for joy and sing the praises of your wonderful name, through Jesus Christ, my Lord. Amen.
