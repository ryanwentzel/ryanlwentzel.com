*A pastoral prayer delivered on January 26, 2025, at Grace Bible Church in Escondido, California.*

Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. We bow before you in reverence and awe. We bow before you in wonder and love and delight. May your wonderful name be honored and praised in our midst today. May it be glorified in us and through us throughout the coming week.

O Father, may your kingdom come. May your will be done, on earth — in Escondido, as in heaven. Rule our hearts by your Word and Spirit. Plant your Word of grace — the gospel of your Son — deep within us. Cause us as a church to be — more and more — a sign and foretaste of your wonderful rule and reign. Extend your love and your grace to our neighbors, to our city, through us. May we be cups of cool water to those who are thirsty. May we be bearers of the good news of your kingdom — both in our words and our deeds.

Likewise, we pray for our missionaries and partners in missions. Make them fruitful in the work of the gospel. Grow your kingdom in and through them. May the nations be glad; may they sing for joy because of the good news of Jesus Christ.

Father, give us this day our daily bread. Thank you for all the ways you’ve provided for us this past week.  Thank you for meeting our many needs. Continue to give us all that we need to live to your glory and praise. Help us to acknowledge you more and more as the fountain of all goodness and grace.

Continue to meet our needs as a church, Lord. Thank you for providing for us in 2024. Do it again in 2025, Lord. And help us to steward the resources you give to this congregation for maximum gospel impact.

Father, this morning we ask you to be a Rock and Refuge to Bill and Marsha, to Bunky and Jadelle, to Marilynn, and to Mike. Be with them in their trials. Comfort them with your presence.

Give peace to our brothers and sisters who live with mental health challenges. Give rest and relief.  Give wisdom and grace to their families. Surround them with compassionate friends.

Father, forgive our debts as we have forgiven our debtors. Our sins and failures are many. More than we even realize. Forgive us. We wander from your ways. We’re selfish and self-absorbed. We indulge in pride and anger and hate. Be merciful to us. Cleanse us from all our sins. Renew us by your Spirit. Teach us to walk in your ways. Assure us of your great love and grace in Jesus Christ.

Lead us not into temptation, heavenly Father. Deliver us from evil and the Evil One. Keep us from yielding to the ugly desires warring in our hearts. Keep us from conforming to the evil ways of this world. Protect us from the Devil’s lies. We can’t stand on our own. We can’t endure in our own strength. Keep us in your love. Guard us by your power — the same power you showed when you raised Jesus from the dead and exalted him to your right hand, far above every other power. 

Father, hear and answer us today because yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever, through Christ our Lord.
