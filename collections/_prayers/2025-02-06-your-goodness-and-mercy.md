Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me\
&emsp;all the days of my life...\
— Psalm 23:6 ESV

O Lord, my faithful shepherd and generous host, help me to believe that your goodness surrounds me and your mercy will not desert me, through Christ our Lord. Amen.
