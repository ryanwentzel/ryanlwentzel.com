Our gracious God and Father in heaven, your name is Steadfast and Faithful. We are weak. We are lonely. Our friends have rejected us. Our enemies attack us with words sharp as swords. But you, O God, have not forsaken us. Continue to be our refuge so that we may praise your wonderful name, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
