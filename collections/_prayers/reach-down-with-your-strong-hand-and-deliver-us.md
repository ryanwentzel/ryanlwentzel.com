O God, you whose steadfast love is higher than the heavens and whose faithfulness knows no end, reach down with your strong hand and deliver us from our enemies; remember that we are your beloved children, so that we may sing songs of thanksgiving to you among our neighbors, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
