Lord God, we are your workmanship in Christ Jesus. You created us and redeemed us for a purpose, but our circumstances often don't make sense to us. They feel pointless, like there's no meaning to what we experience. Help us to fix our hope on you, and assure us of your steadfast love, so that we might do the good you've purposed for us, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
