Are you interested in studying 1 & 2 Samuel? Looking for resources to consult for a sermon series. Here are the books and commentaries I've found helpful in my preaching and teaching.

- [1 and 2 Samuel: An Introduction and Commentary](https://amzn.to/4hGj0Pt) by V. Philips Long. Part of the *Tyndale Old Testament Commentary* series. Concise (something I value) but packed with exegetical insight. I typically consulted this commentary first.
- [The First Book of Samuel](https://amzn.to/3Q29rhX) and [The Second Book of Samuel](https://amzn.to/40GxAzh) by David Toshio Tsumura. Part of the *New International Commentary on the Old Testament* series. More technical than the commentary by Long. Highlights the literary structure and flow of the books.
- [The Hebrew Bible: A Translation with Commentary](https://amzn.to/42AUF9c) by Robert Alter. Alter's work on the Hebrew Bible has been called "a masterpiece of deep learning and fine sensibility." Both his translation and notes on the text are always worth consulting.
- [1 Samuel: Looking on the Heart](https://amzn.to/40XOJ8Y) and [2 Samuel: Out of Every Adversity](https://amzn.to/42DGoJ8) by Dale Ralph Davis. Reformed. Expository. Especially helpful to preachers.
- [Leap Over a Wall : Earthy Spirituality for Everyday Christians](https://amzn.to/42G7QFZ) by Eugene Peterson. Peterson was a poet-pastor. His reflections on David's life and its relevance to contemporary Christians are marked by a simple beauty.
