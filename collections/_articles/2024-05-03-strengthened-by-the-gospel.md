*The following is from a [sermon]({% link _sermons/2024-03-19-it-all-ends-in-praise.md %}) I preached on Romans 16:25–27.*

Paul begins the doxology by highlighting God's power in the gospel. Look at what he says in verse 25: <q cite="Romans 16:25">Now to him who is able.</q> That's power language. <q>To him who has the power.</q> Power to do what? <q cite="Romans 16:25">To strengthen you according to my gospel.</q>
