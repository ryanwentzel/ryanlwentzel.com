In my head, wrapping its sinuous body around my brain. Squeezing, tighter and tighter and ...

A blizzard, maybe. I don't know. We don't have those where I live, but I've seen them on TV. Cold down in your bones. Wind like the coyotes on the edge of the neighborhood after dark. Visibility low. Can't see. Where you're going or where you've been, either. Alone. Lost. Tired. So painfully tired.

Light-headed. Confused. Why can't I think? Try harder. Concentrate. Try. Maybe I should stop thinking? I can't. Stop thinking, I mean. Just for a moment. Just to catch my breath. Why can't I stop? My legs. Move them, I've got to. Right now, run! Get away! But I can't. So tired, so painfully tired.

Sinking. Lower and lower. How much further? Into the deep, into the abyss, this dark abyss.
