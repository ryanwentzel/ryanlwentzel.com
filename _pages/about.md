I am a husband, father of five, and a pastor at [Grace Bible Church](https://grcbible.org/ "A Reformed Baptist church in Escondido, CA") (GBC) in Escondido, CA.  

I have been a pastor at GBC since 2009. Before joining the GBC staff full-time in 2016, I worked as a software engineer at a large biotech company in San Diego. My primary roles at GBC include preaching, teaching, and pastoral care.

Many of my sermons can be found at [SermonAudio.com](https://www.sermonaudio.com/search.asp?speakeronly=true&currsection=sermonsspeaker&keyword=Ryan_Wentzel "Listen to Ryan Wentzel's sermons at SermonAudio.com"){:target="_blank"} or on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UChDSSOO_z11aGFaFKVCbGlg "Watch Ryan Wentzel's sermon on YouTube"){:target="_blank"}.
